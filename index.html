Why we exist

Our Mission

We build markets for Amazon foods so that the forest is worth more standing than cleared. Native communities harvest, we process and export, and fair prices flow back up the river.

When a family can earn a living from macambo trees, those trees stay in the ground.

Eco Ola, Iquitos

Partnership with native communities

We buy directly from Indigenous and riverside communities in Loreto, agreeing prices up front and paying on delivery, so harvest income stays where the work is done.

Regenerative agroforestry

Our products come from wild trees and mixed forest gardens. With Acate Amazon Conservation we help communities replant native species on degraded land.

Women-led work

Women lead much of the harvesting, sorting and processing behind our products, and we design our sourcing so that this work is paid and recognized.

Traceable quality

Every lot is processed under HACCP controls in Iquitos and ships with a certificate of analysis, so buyers know exactly what they receive.
